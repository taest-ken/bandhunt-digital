WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?

Fred Again.. is heading to India for a massive tour. But instead of just 
dropping tickets online like every other boring concert, we're building a 
3-month episodic digital scavenger hunt called "THE HITMAP".

It's a play on a "heat map"—except instead of tracking temperature, fans track 
down Fred's secret hiding spots across Indian cities using BEATS and music HITS.

The entire experience is divided into three high-energy phases that blur the line 
between a video game on your phone and a real-life treasure hunt in the streets.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: THE DIGITAL HITMAP (The Online Pre-Hunt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the browser game fans play on their phones and laptops in the days 
leading up to an actual ticket drop.

1. THE LOOK & FEEL:
   You open the site and see a dark, covert, satellite-style map of the city 
   (like Mumbai or Delhi). Right in the center is an interactive tactical compass.

2. HOW YOU MOVE (THE JOYSTICK COMPASS):
   The compass works just like an analog thumbstick on a PlayStation or Xbox 
   controller. You touch it with your finger and drag it in any direction.
   - Pull it slightly: The map slowly crawls for precise neighborhood searching.
   - Pull it to the outer rim: The map pans smoothly across the city at a 
     controlled cruising speed (speed is capped so you can't just cheat by 
     spinning the map wildly).
   - Let go: The compass springs smoothly back to the center while the map 
     glides to a cushioned stop.

3. HUNTING WITH YOUR EARS (THE CORE GAME):
   A Fred Again track is playing in your headphones, and you use your ears like a 
   sonar radar:
   
   * COLD ZONE (Far Away):
     The song sounds heavily muffled and underwater, like you're standing outside 
     a nightclub listening through three feet of solid concrete.
     The compass glows an icy, freezing CYAN (#00F0FF).
     A sharp needle inside the compass points in the direction where Fred is 
     hiding (with subtle radio static jitter when you're super far).

   * GETTING WARMER (Closing In):
     As you steer the map toward the target, the music starts breaking through! 
     The drums get punchier and the highs get clearer.
     The compass smoothly shifts colors—from icy cyan to electric mint, lime, 
     and bright neon yellow.

   * HOT ZONE (Target Vicinity):
     The track starts blasting with deep sub-bass and crisp vocals.
     The compass shifts from warm amber to a piercing, strobe-pulsing CRIMSON RED.

4. THE TARGET LOCK-IN & PRIZE:
   When you steer the compass directly over Fred's secret coordinates (within 25 
   meters), it locks in! 
   The compass morphs into a glowing, pulsing "Fred Blip".
   Tapping the blip unlocks:
   - A short, lo-fi 2D cartoon loop showing Fred doing something funny in that 
     exact neighborhood (e.g. hauling giant flower garlands at Dadar Market, 
     sipping roadside cutting chai, or playing a mini keyboard on a crowded local 
     train).
   - Lore text and clues about the upcoming real-life drop.

5. THE GLOBAL CLOCK:
   The game is synchronized for everyone in the world. When the on-screen 
   countdown timer hits zero, Fred moves to a brand new hiding spot with a brand 
   new song, and the entire community starts hunting again simultaneously!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2: "THE BANDHUNT IS LIVE NOW!" (The Real-World Street Drop)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After a few days of online hunting, the countdown hits zero and the site 
transitions into an emergency street-level event.

1. THE ALERT:
   The entire website flashes into a high-voltage, red-alert CRT mode with 
   pulsing scanlines.
   The audio cuts into a "pirate radio intercept"—a high-energy Fred beat mixed 
   with walkie-talkie beeps, police scanner static, and crowd chatter.

2. THE CALL TO ACTION:
   A massive headline takes over the screen:
   "PUT YOUR PHONE DOWN. GET OUT ONTO THE PAVEMENT. 
    IF YOU'RE READING THIS SCREEN, YOU'RE ALREADY LOSING."

3. REAL-LIFE HUNTING:
   The game is no longer on your screen—it's happening in real life!
   Fred's team has hidden real concert passes and glowing wristbands on the 
   actual physical streets of the city (tucked behind market stalls, historical 
   monuments, or secret street corners).
   The online map displays glowing sonar pulse rings over active drop 
   neighborhoods and posts live crowd sighting alerts so fans know which 
   neighborhoods to sprint toward before all passes are claimed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3: THE WAITING ROOM & RECAP (Between Cities)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once all tickets are found in that city, the offline hunt ends and the web app 
enters the "Waiting Room" while Fred travels to the next city on the tour route.

1. THE NEXT CITY TEASER:
   The map automatically pans and locks onto the next city (e.g. shifting from 
   Mumbai to New Delhi), with an ambient neon radar beam looping across the city.
   A teaser reads:
   "BANDHUNT PHASE 2 COMING TO NEW DELHI SOON. PLAY PHASE 1 RECAP TO SEE WHAT 
    WENT DOWN IN MUMBAI."

2. THE RECAP MOVIE:
   Fans can tap a massive "PLAY RECON LOG" button to watch a 30 to 45-second, 
   super high-energy video montage.
   This video is cut from REAL OFFLINE FOOTAGE of the actual street hunt that just 
   happened—hundreds of fans sprinting down alleys, digging up passes, screaming 
   with excitement, and Fred's surprise pop-up DJ set in the middle of the crowd, 
   all edited with kinetic cuts landing on every beat of a Fred Again drop.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS EXPERIENCE IS SO POWERFUL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. It turns a boring map into a musical video game where you navigate with your 
   ears rather than your eyes.
2. It has an unbroken, fluid color spectrum that reacts in real time at 60 frames 
   per second.
3. It takes online hype and translates it directly into real-world culture, 
   getting kids off their couches and racing through the city with their friends.
4. It treats the tour drop like an underground cultural event rather than a 
   corporate ticket sale.

PART 1: THE GOLDEN CHEAT CODE — HOW TO MAKE ANY IMAGE WORK ON WEB
================================================================================
You don't need web design experience. Just keep these 3 simple rules in mind:

1. USE TRANSPARENT PNGs (PNG-24):
   - Whenever you export a sticker, compass needle, or character, turn off the 
     background layer so it is transparent.
   - This lets your artwork float cleanly on top of the live map!

2. WORK ON A SQUARE CANVAS (1000 x 1000 pixels):
   - In Procreate, Canva, or Photoshop, make a square canvas.
   - Center your artwork with a little breathing room around the edges.

3. POINT DIRECTIONAL ITEMS STRAIGHT "UP":
   - For the compass needle/arrow or any character pointing, have it facing 
     straight UP (12 o'clock / North) in its default state.
   - Our code will automatically rotate your PNG in 360 degrees in real time to 
     point towards Fred!

PART 3: THE COMPASS (HEAD & NEEDLE ASSETS)
================================================================================
The compass is the centerpiece of the sound game. When users touch and drag it 
with their thumb, the head slides across the screen while the needle rotates in 
360 degrees to point toward Fred's secret coordinate!

To give it the best tactile feel, please provide **2 SEPARATE TRANSPARENT PNGs**:

[ ] 3.1. File 1: The Compass Base / Head (compass_head.png):
    - This is the puck or circular base that the user touches and drags around.
    - It moves with the user's thumb, but stays right-side up (does not spin).
    - Format: Square canvas (500x500 or 1000x1000 px), transparent .PNG.
    - Ideas: A custom base ring, an auto-rickshaw meter base, a vinyl record, 
      a chai saucer, or a colorful tactile joystick puck.

[ ] 3.2. File 2: The Directional Needle / Arrow (compass_needle.png):
    - This sits on top of the head and **spins dynamically in 360 degrees** in 
      real time to point directly towards Fred!
    - Format: Square canvas (500x500 or 1000x1000 px), transparent .PNG.
    - IMPORTANT: Draw this pointing straight UP (12 o'clock / North) in its 
      default position so our code can calculate the exact rotation angle!
    - Ideas: A chunky doodle arrow, a cartoon Fred head with sunglasses looking 
      forward, an auto-rickshaw facing forward, a finger pointing, or a sneaker.

*(Note: If you prefer a single character that both moves and spins all at once, 
you can send just 1 PNG facing straight UP, but having the Head and Needle as 2 
separate files gives the slickest tactile video game feel!)*
